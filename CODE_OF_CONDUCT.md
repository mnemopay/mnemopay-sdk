# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

MnemoPay is the trust + reputation substrate for AI agents. We hold contributors to the same standard: act in good faith, be specific, and don't waste each other's time.

**Expected behavior:**

- Use welcoming and inclusive language. Critique code, not the person who wrote it.
- Be respectful of differing experience levels, viewpoints, and the time of unpaid maintainers.
- Open with the question or the problem statement before the proposed solution.
- Disagree with reasoning, not with reputation. Cite the line of code, the spec, or the data.
- Accept that maintainers may say no to a PR for reasons not visible to you (compliance, roadmap, supply-chain risk).

**Not tolerated:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, sexualized language, deliberate intimidation, or stalking, in issues / PRs / Discord / any project-affiliated channel.
- Posting other people's private information (real-world identity, address, employer, financial information) without explicit permission.
- Dismissive or condescending replies that exist to make the responder look smart rather than to help the asker.
- Repeated low-effort "drive-by" PRs that ignore [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md) (no issue first, no tests, no rationale).

## Scope

This Code applies in all project-affiliated spaces: GitHub repos under the `mnemopay` org, the `@mnemopay/sdk` npm package issues, the dashboard / console support channels, and any social-media account using the MnemoPay brand. It also applies when an individual is representing the project at a public event, conference, or on a podcast.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is **Jeremiah Omiagbo** (J&B Enterprise LLC).

Report violations to **info@getbizsuite.com** with subject `[coc] <short description>`. Include links to the offending content if possible. We will respond within 5 business days.

Possible outcomes, scaling by severity and repeat-offense pattern:

1. **Private warning.** Most common. A one-time explanation of which behavior crossed the line.
2. **Public clarification.** When the offending behavior happened in public and a private warning would leave bystanders thinking the behavior was acceptable.
3. **Temporary ban** from project spaces (1 to 30 days, at the maintainer's discretion).
4. **Permanent ban** for serious or repeated offenses, or any single instance of harassment, doxxing, or threats.

Decisions are final. There is no formal appeal process — this is a small project with one maintainer, not a foundation with a board.

## Maintainer commitment

The maintainer agrees to:

- Apply this code consistently regardless of contributor seniority, employer, or relationship.
- Recuse from enforcement actions where they have an undisclosed conflict of interest.
- Keep enforcement reports confidential unless the reporter consents to disclosure or the case becomes legally compelled.
